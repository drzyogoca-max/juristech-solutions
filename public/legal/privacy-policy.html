Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At JurisTech Solutions, we take your privacy and the security of your data seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and protect your information.

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you:

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service.
  • To notify you about changes to our Service.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To gather analysis or valuable information so that we can improve our Service.
  • To monitor the usage of our Service and detect, prevent, and address technical issues.

3. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights regarding your personal information under the CCPA:

  • Right to Know: You can request details about the personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Right to Delete: You can request the deletion of your personal information that we have collected.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You have the right to opt-out of the sale of your personal information. (Note: JurisTech Solutions does NOT sell your personal information).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at legal@juristech.solutions.

4. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have data protection rights under GDPR, including the right to access, update, or delete the information we have on you; the right of rectification; the right to object; the right of restriction; the right to data portability; and the right to withdraw consent.

6. Data Security

The security of your data is paramount. We implement commercially acceptable means, including AES-256 encryption and SOC 2 compliant infrastructure, to protect your Personal Data. However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Data Deletion and Retention

We will retain your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. You can request the complete deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting our support team.
