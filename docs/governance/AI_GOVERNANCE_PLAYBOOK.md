## 1. Executive Summary & Core Governance Principles

JurisTech Solutions operates on a strict **Human-in-the-Loop Sovereign Architecture**. The artificial intelligence engines, multi-agent swarms, and cross-border simulation modules act as high-precision analytical instruments under the authoritative supervision of human legal counsel.

### Core Inviolable Tenets
1. **No Autonomous External Legal Actions**: AI agents synthesize insights and probability matrices but are strictly prohibited from dispatching binding external legal actions or filings autonomously.
2. **Zero Raw Document Persistence**: Sovereign VPC adapters, knowledge meshes, and compliance proof oracles exchange abstract vector representations and cryptographic hashes only.
3. **Immutability of Commercial & Financial Systems**: Payment gateways (Paddle, Stripe, Fawry, Mada, SWIFT) and financial database ledgers remain permanently isolated from autonomous AI execution paths.
4. **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Approval**: Every contract state progression in the Smart Legal Contract Fabric and every multi-agent swarm synthesis requires authenticated human counsel sign-off (`SWARM_SYNTHESIS_COMPLETE_PENDING_GATE`).

## 3. Human Approval Gate Protocol

When an AI swarm completes an analysis for an enterprise transaction (e.g., cross-border M&A, high-liability EPC agreement, or regulatory filing simulation):
1. **Synthesis Phase**: 5 specialized agents (Research, Compliance, Contract Analysis, Risk, Audit) generate a consensus risk index.
2. **Quorum Verification**: The orchestrator checks inter-agent consensus score (minimum threshold $\ge 95\%$).
3. **Pending Gate Intercept**: State is set to `SWARM_SYNTHESIS_COMPLETE_PENDING_GATE`.
4. **Counsel Authorization**: Authenticated Enterprise Administrator or General Counsel reviews the abstract findings and explicitly approves or dismisses the action.
5. **Cryptographic Anchoring**: Lattice-based post-quantum signature is generated and anchored to the audit record.
