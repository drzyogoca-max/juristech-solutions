## 1. Constitutional Purpose
This Charter establishes the constitutional foundation for the **JurisTech Solutions Institutional Intelligence Marketplace & Governed Legal Exchange Fabric** (`v29.0.0`).

The Marketplace operates as an open, neutral institutional trust exchange where sovereign law firms, corporate legal entities, arbitration chambers, and regulatory bodies discover, verify, and interact with specialized legal intelligence capabilities.

---

## 2. Inviolable Guarantees
1. **Marketplace Neutrality**: The system shall never apply algorithmic blacklisting, preferential exclusion, or hidden ranking bias against participating legal institutions (`marketplaceNeutralityRequired = true`).
2. **Non-Self-Certification**: Passports and credentials are never self-issued by JurisTech Solutions; they represent multi-party verifiable cryptographic attestations (`NO_SELF_ISSUED_PASSPORT = true`).
3. **Zero Private Document Storage**: Under no circumstances shall raw contracts, legal briefs, or confidential customer documents be stored or traded within the marketplace fabric (`zeroPrivateDocumentStorage = true`).
4. **Stateless Governance Integration**: Enterprise integrations with SAP, Oracle, and GRC platforms operate in a strictly stateless mode (`statelessVerificationOnly = true`).
5. **Auditor Sees Proof Not Data**: External regulatory and compliance auditors verify compliance exclusively through Zero-Knowledge Proofs without accessing customer data (`AUDITOR_SEES_PROOF_NOT_DATA = true`).

---

## 3. Human Sovereignty & Authority
Every trust passport issuance, regulatory alert explanation, and institutional verification record requires explicit human legal counsel authorization. Automated systems provide suggestions and cryptographic proofs; human authority remains absolute.
