## 2. Inviolable Governance Principles

### Principle 1: Sovereign In-Country Node Residency
Each jurisdictional node (Saudi Arabia, UAE, European Union, Singapore) operates within strictly isolated sovereign compute boundaries. Cross-border communication is strictly limited to Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP) verification tokens (`ZERO_UNENCRYPTED_EGRESS = true`).

### Principle 2: Advisory Synthesis & Exclusive Human Legal Authority
The Autonomous Institutional Synthesis Engine operates strictly as an advisory intelligence layer. It never issues binding legal conclusions or automated decisions. All syntheses require human legal counsel sign-off (`NO_AUTONOMOUS_SYNTHESIS_DECISION = true`).

### Principle 3: Proof Settlement vs Financial Isolation
The Institutional Trust Settlement Ledger settles mathematical compliance proofs and verifiable certificates only. Financial transactions and payment gateways remain 100% frozen under Rule Zero (`NO_FINANCIAL_SETTLEMENT = true`).

### Principle 4: Continuous External Assurance Without Exposure
External accredited auditors verify continuous compliance via Zero-Knowledge Proof streams without gaining access to confidential customer contracts or private documents (`AUDITOR_SEES_PROOF_NOT_DATA = true`).
