## 1. Institutional Governance Mandate
This charter defines the operational and regulatory boundaries governing the Institutional Legal Operating System Layer across all corporate holdings, sovereign subsidiaries, and SPVs managed by JurisTech Solutions.

---

## 2. Inviolable Institutional Safeguards 🔒
1. **Coordination & Telemetry Only**: The Operating System functions strictly as a coordination, visibility, and telemetry platform. It possesses zero authority to autonomously modify corporate bylaws, change board resolutions, execute commercial contracts, or alter financial ledgers (`INSTITUTIONAL_COORDINATION_ONLY = true`).
2. **Zero Cross-Tenant Contamination**: Strict logical and cryptographic enclave isolation guarantees that multi-entity holding data is never merged or cross-contaminated (`ZERO_TENANT_DATA_CROSS_CONTAMINATION = true`).
3. **Mandatory Human Executive Countersignatures**: All institutional policy adoptions and lifecycle changes require dual countersignatures from the General Counsel and relevant Chief Executive Officer (`EXECUTIVE_OFFICER_SIGN_OFF_REQUIRED = true`).
