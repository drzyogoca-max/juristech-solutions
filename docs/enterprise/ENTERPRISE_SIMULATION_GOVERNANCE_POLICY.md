**Target Release**: `v23.0.0 — Global Enterprise Intelligence & Simulation Layer`  
**Effective Date**: `August 26, 2026`  
**Guiding Invariant**: `AI advises. Humans decide. Systems enforce boundaries.`  

---

## 1. Institutional Mandate
This policy governs all What-If policy impact simulations, cross-border regulatory forecasting models, and algorithmic risk assessments conducted on JurisTech Solutions.

---

## 2. Inviolable Simulation Guardrails 🔒
1. **Simulation Sandbox Isolation**: All simulation models operate strictly within an isolated sandbox environment (`SIMULATION_SANDBOX_ONLY = true`).
2. **Prohibition of Autonomous Execution**: Simulations cannot modify active enterprise policies, trigger external system actions, execute purchases, or alter customer pricing (`NO_AUTONOMOUS_POLICY_EXECUTION = true`).
3. **Zero Simulation Payload Retention**: Scenario input matrices and hypothetical customer contract conditions are strictly ephemeral with zero persistence (`ZERO_SIMULATION_PAYLOAD_RETENTION = true`).
4. **Mandatory Dual Human Approval**: Any policy adjustments informed by simulation outputs require co-signatures from the General Counsel and relevant Chief Officer (`DUAL_EXECUTIVE_COUNTERSIGNATURE = true`).
