# Enterprise Global Legal Benchmark & Data Isolation Charter 📜📊
**Charter Identifier**: `JUR-CHR-BNCH-2026-V23`  
**Target Release**: `v23.0.0 — Global Enterprise Intelligence & Simulation Layer`  
**Supervising Officers**: `Chief Risk Officer & General Counsel`  

---

## 1. Purpose & Guiding Principles
The Global Legal Benchmark Charter guarantees that cross-sector comparative indices operate strictly on aggregated statistical telemetry without ingesting, exposing, or processing individual client data.

---

## 2. Inviolable Benchmark Safeguards
- **Zero Individual Customer Identifiers**: No client names, tenant IDs, matter numbers, or proprietary identifiers are included in benchmark datasets (`NO_INDIVIDUAL_CUSTOMER_IDENTIFIERS = true`).
- **Aggregated Observability Only**: Benchmark scores reflect macro-level sector turnaround velocities and statutory alignment indices (`AGGREGATE_METRICS_ONLY = true`).
- **No Raw Document Exposure**: Benchmark engines are prohibited from accessing or storing raw contract text (`NO_RAW_CONTRACT_EXPOSURE = true`).
- **Advisory Improvement Roadmaps**: Sector maturity indices serve strictly as constructive advisory roadmaps, with zero automated tier demotions (`NO_AUTOMATED_TIER_DEMOTION = true`).
