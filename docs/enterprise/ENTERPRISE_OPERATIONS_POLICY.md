## 1. Executive Purpose & Governance Mandate
This policy governs the operational orchestration, real-time trust telemetry, institutional contract milestone governance, and empirical business value realization models within the JurisTech Solutions Platform.

## 2. Inviolable Guardrails (Rule Zero Preserved)
1. **Operations Orchestration Only**: KPI engines and resilience monitors function in analytical mode only (`OPERATIONS_ORCHESTRATION_ONLY = true`).
2. **Telemetry Without Customer Payload Exposure**: Real-time heartbeat signals track latency and subsystem health without capturing customer prompts or payloads (`ZERO_CUSTOMER_PAYLOAD_EXPOSURE = true`).
3. **Contract Lifecycle State Tracking Only**: Milestones record cryptographically verifiable timestamps without storing raw document text (`ZERO_RAW_CONTRACT_RETENTION = true`).
4. **Estimated Value Quantification**: ROI models provide empirical estimation only, requiring dual validation (`ESTIMATION_AND_QUANTIFICATION_ONLY = true`, `DUAL_EXECUTIVE_VALIDATION = true`).
5. **Zero Financial Gateway Mutation**: Payment gateways (Paddle, Stripe, Fawry, Mada, SWIFT) and database tables remain 100% frozen.

## 3. Dual Sign-Off Governance Requirement
Official annual value reports require dual sign-off from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and General Counsel (GC).
