## 1. Global Ecosystem Principles
The Global Legal Ecosystem Charter establishes the federated trust architecture uniting certified law firms, specialized arbitration boutiques, and regulatory advisory councils across sovereign jurisdictions.

---

## 2. Inviolable Federation Guardrails 🔒
1. **Zero Client Data Exposure**: Certified partners access only anonymized, structured collaboration scopes. Raw client documents and contracts are never transmitted or exposed (`ZERO_CLIENT_DATA_EXPOSURE = true`).
2. **Dual Human Engagement Authorization**: No external law firm or partner node is engaged without explicit countersignatures from corporate counsel (`DUAL_HUMAN_ENGAGEMENT_APPROVAL = true`).
3. **Transparent & Dispute-Reviewable Trust Marketplace**: Competency ratings must always be backed by verifiable credentials with full explanatory rationale, prohibiting opaque algorithmic blacklisting (`NO_ALGORITHMIC_BLACKLISTING = true`, `HUMAN_REVIEW_FOR_TRUST_DISPUTES = true`).
