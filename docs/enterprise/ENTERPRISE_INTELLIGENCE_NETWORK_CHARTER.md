## 1. Federated Legal Knowledge Graph Protocol

- `NO_AUTONOMOUS_LEGAL_INTERPRETATION = true`
- `NO_AUTONOMOUS_LEGAL_REASONING = true`

## 2. Inviolable Governance & Non-Self-Certification
- **No Self-Certification**: All compliance proofs are verifiable attestations backed by human legal officer signatures (`NO_SELF_CERTIFICATION = true`).
- **Zero Document Storage**: The network preserves mathematical hashes and cryptographic proofs, with strict prohibition of client document persistence (`ZERO_PRIVATE_DOCUMENT_STORAGE = true`).
- **Observability Without Surveillance**: Network telemetry monitors performance without inspecting contract payloads.
