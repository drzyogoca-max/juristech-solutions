# Enterprise Partner Ecosystem & Channel Governance Policy 🏛️🤝
**Document Identifier**: `JUR-POL-ECO-2026-V22`  
**Target Release**: `v22.0.0 — Enterprise Ecosystem & Partner Network`  
**Effective Date**: `August 26, 2026`  
**Classification**: `Institutional Executive Directive`  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy establishes rigorous governance standards for certified law firms, system integrators, sovereign distributors, and channel partners integrating with or distributing JurisTech Solutions.

---

## 2. Inviolable Partner Ecosystem Guardrails 🔒
1. **Mandatory Human Dual Approval**: No partner or channel reseller may be approved or onboarded autonomously by AI systems (`NO_PARTNER_AUTONOMOUS_APPROVAL = true`, `DUAL_HUMAN_PARTNER_ONBOARDING_APPROVAL = true`).
2. **Zero Partner Data Leakage**: Partner organizational telemetry and client deployment counts are strictly quarantined; zero customer data or proprietary contract text is shared with partner entities (`ZERO_PARTNER_DATA_LEAKAGE = true`).
3. **No Automated Commercial Commitments**: Telemetry indices and partner readiness scores do not constitute legally binding commercial warranties or exclusivity contracts (`NO_PARTNER_COMMERCIAL_COMMITMENT = true`).
4. **Continuous SLA Verification**: All accredited partner alliances are subject to continuous SLA compliance audits (target $\ge 99.5\%$ availability).

---

## 3. Executive Supervision
Onboarding of Tier-1 Sovereign Strategic Partners requires co-signatures from the General Counsel (GC) and Chief Ecosystem Officer (CEcO).
