## 1. Purpose & Sovereign Respect
The Cross-Border Governance Federation Policy establishes the principles of multi-jurisdictional statutory harmony across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, the UK, the EU, Singapore, and the United States.

---

## 2. Inviolable Federation Guardrails
- **Sovereign Statutory Respect**: In-country statutory regulations always take absolute precedence over transnational harmonized guidelines (`SOVEREIGN_POLICY_RESPECT_ENFORCED = true`).
- **No Automated Policy Overrides**: The system is prohibited from overriding local statutory constraints or automatedly altering contractual governing law clauses (`NO_AUTONOMOUS_POLICY_OVERRIDE = true`).
- **Continuous Audit Observability**: Compliance observability across ISO 42001, ISO 27001, SOC 2, SAMA CSF, and PDPL operates continuously via cryptographic hashing without ingesting raw customer contracts (`ZERO_RAW_DOCUMENT_INSPECTION = true`).
