## 1. Principles of Sovereign Institutional Certification
1. **Strict Prohibition of Self-Accreditation**: JurisTech Solutions strictly shall not issue certifications or attestations to itself (`NO_SELF_ACCREDITATION = true`).
2. **Accreditation Authority Supremacy**: Certifications are issued and validated exclusively by recognized state regulators, ministries of justice, bar associations, and ISO-accredited certifying bodies.
3. **Six-Stage Certification Lifecycle**:
   - Stage 1: `Institution Request`
   - Stage 2: `External Authority Verification`
   - Stage 3: `Cryptographic Attestation`
   - Stage 4: `Human Signoff & Approval`
   - Stage 5: `Active Operational Certification`
   - Stage 6: `Continuous Renewal & Revocation`
4. **Immutable Registry Ledger**: All institutional credentials are maintained in an immutable SHA-512 cryptographic ledger.
