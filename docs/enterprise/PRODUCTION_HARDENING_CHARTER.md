## 1. Executive Purpose

The **Institutional Production Hardening Charter** ratifies the operational governance framework for **JurisTech Solutions v33.1.0**. It formalizes the telemetry, incident escalation, evidence lifecycle, and progressive sandbox graduation standards that ensure planetary-scale stability, deterministic predictability, and absolute customer data privacy.

---

## 2. Inviolable Governance Pillars

1. **Production Observability & SLA Telemetry**:
   - Availability is continuously tracked against a **99.99% SLA**.
   - Zero customer document content or proprietary legal work product is ever stored or parsed in telemetry streams (`NO_CUSTOMER_DOCUMENT_STORAGE = true`).
2. **Enterprise Incident Escalation Boundary**:
   - For all **P1 Critical** and **P2 High** operational incidents, AI provides recommendations and forensic synthesis, but **only named human commanders** have authority to sign off on incident closure (`NO_AUTONOMOUS_P1_RESOLUTION = true`).
3. **Compliance Evidence Lifecycle State Machine**:
   - Evidence assets transition strictly across 5 deterministic states:
     $$\text{CREATED} \longrightarrow \text{VALIDATED} \longrightarrow \text{EXPIRED} \longrightarrow \text{RENEWED} \longrightarrow \text{ARCHIVED}$$
   - Retroactive alteration of past state transitions or cryptographic hashes is strictly impossible (`NO_RETROACTIVE_EVIDENCE_MUTATION = true`).
   - Evidence assets represent auditable cryptographic proof bundles, never self-issued legal licenses (`EVIDENCE_ASSET_NOT_CERTIFICATION = true`).
4. **Progressive Sandbox Graduation Program**:
   - Tenants progress exclusively via the 3-tier sequence:
     $$\text{Academic Pilot} \longrightarrow \text{Regulated Enterprise Pilot} \longrightarrow \text{Production Tenant}$$
   - Direct jumping from sandbox to production is blocked by architectural policy (`ZERO_DIRECT_SANDBOX_TO_PRODUCTION = true`).
   - Every graduation tier mandates signed human General Counsel approval (`GENERAL_COUNSEL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED = true`).
5. **Rule Zero Preservation**:
   - Zero database schema migrations (`TASK_41_DATABASE_MIGRATION = 0`).
   - Payment gateways (Paddle, Stripe, Fawry, Mada) remain 100% frozen (`TASK_41_PAYMENT_CHANGE = false`).
