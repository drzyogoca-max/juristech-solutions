**Target Release**: `v25.0.0 — Global Legal Intelligence Ecosystem & Silver Jubilee`

---

## 1. Scope & Framework Harmony
This policy codifies the requirements for assessing and certifying enterprise AI systems in compliance with ISO/IEC 42001:2023, the European Union AI Act, and the SDAIA AI Ethics Principles.

---

## 2. Mandatory Verification Invariants
- **Non-Automated Certification Issuance**: AI conformity certificates and governance seals require explicit, verified human executive approval (`NO_AUTOMATED_CERTIFICATION_ISSUANCE = true`, `CERTIFICATION_HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED = true`).
- **Cryptographic Audit Proofs**: All assessments generate immutable SHA-512 cryptographic digests without persisting raw model training payloads or system memory (`ZERO_SYSTEM_PAYLOAD_RETENTION = true`).
- **Mandatory Expiry & Recertification**: Every governance certificate includes an enforceable validity timestamp requiring annual recertification (`CERTIFICATION_EXPIRY_REQUIRED = true`).
