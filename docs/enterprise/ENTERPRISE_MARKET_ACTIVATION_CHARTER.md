## 1. Governance Principles of Institutional Market Activation
1. **Governed Tenancy Isolation**: All enterprise pilots and sandboxes are memory-isolated with strictly zero cross-tenant contamination or production leakage (`ISOLATED_ENTERPRISE_TENANCY = true`).
2. **Zero Automatic Production Promotion**: No tenant can transition from sandbox to production autonomously; explicit human General Counsel signoff is mandatory (`ZERO_AUTOMATIC_PRODUCTION_PROMOTION = true`).
3. **Evidence Assets Not Certifications**: JurisTech Solutions provides cryptographic ZKP evidence and audit logs, not self-issued legal licenses or regulatory certifications (`EVIDENCE_ASSET_NOT_CERTIFICATION = true`).
4. **Partner Neutrality**: Academic institutions, audit firms, and sovereign cloud providers possess equal standing with zero commercial influence on AI routing or paid prioritization (`NO_PARTNER_INFLUENCE_ON_AI_ROUTING = true`, `NO_PAY_TO_RANK_PARTNER_PRIORITY = true`).
5. **Non-Transactional Economic Telemetry**: Enterprise SaaS metrics (ARR, CAC, Gross Margin, ROI) are computed statelessly in memory with zero database writes and 100% frozen payment gateways (`NO_AUTONOMOUS_FINANCIAL_DECISION = true`).
