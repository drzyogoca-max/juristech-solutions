**Charter Identifier**: `JUR-CHR-REV-GOV-2026-V21`  
**Target Release**: `v21.0.0 — Commercial Intelligence & Customer Trust`  
**Supervising Officers**: `Chief Financial Officer & General Counsel`  

---

## 1. Institutional Mandate
The Enterprise Revenue Governance Charter establishes strict operational boundaries separating revenue telemetry from automated decision-making and client privacy.

---

## 2. Mandatory Operational Principles
- **Principle 1: Transparent Assumptions**: Every revenue projection must disclose its underlying mathematical assumptions and confidence interval (`FORECAST_ASSUMPTION_DISCLOSURE = true`).
- **Principle 2: Cryptographic Auditability**: Revenue and adoption digests must be stamped with SHA-512 cryptographic evidence hashes.
- **Principle 3: Privacy Preservation**: Account intelligence gathers only aggregate velocity metrics and seat health; zero client payload data is logged or retained (`ZERO_SENSITIVE_PAYLOAD_LOGGING = true`).
- **Principle 4: Payment Isolation**: Core payment gateways and financial ledger schemas remain strictly frozen and isolated from commercial intelligence algorithms.
