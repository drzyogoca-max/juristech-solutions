## 1. Executive Purpose & Scope
This policy mandates the governance framework for JurisTech Solutions' external enterprise customer adoption, automated RFP/RFX intelligence acceleration, global regulatory passports, and cross-border expansion blueprints.

---

## 2. Inviolable Governance Principles

### 2.1 Rule Zero Financial & Data Immutability
- **Payment & Pricing Freeze**: Paddle, Stripe, Fawry, Mada, and SWIFT configurations remain strictly isolated and immutable.
- **Zero Raw Document Retention**: `Proof Generated != Data Stored`. No customer contracts, privileged briefs, or confidential RFP attachments are stored in persistent databases.

### 2.2 Dual-Key Executive Attestation
- All global market blueprints and regulatory passport evidence packages require dual digital authorization:
  1. **General Counsel (GC)**: Regulatory and jurisdictional sovereignty validation.
  2. **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)**: Cryptographic integrity and subprocessor risk attestation.

### 2.3 Non-Autonomous Bound
- The Enterprise Adoption Engine and Partner Governance Fabric operate under **Read-Only / Decision-Support Only** modes. No autonomous vendor blocking or automated binding contract submission is permitted without explicit human approval.
