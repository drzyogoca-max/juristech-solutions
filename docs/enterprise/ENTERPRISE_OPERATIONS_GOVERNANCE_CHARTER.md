# JurisTech Solutions — Enterprise Operations Governance Charter 📜⚖️
**Charter Reference**: `JUR-CHR-OPS-GOV-2026-V20`
**Status**: `ACTIVE & MANDATED (v20.0.0)`
**Signatories**: General Counsel (GC), Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

---

## 1. Operational KPI Boundaries
- Operational metrics measure system velocity, SLA adherence, and regulatory compliance pre-emption.
- No automated business changes or unilateral contractual commitments are permitted.

## 2. Telemetry and Privacy Safeguards
- Telemetry monitors latency, uptime, and drift pre-emption.
- No user PII or sensitive legal drafting content is retained or transmitted during telemetry operations.

## 3. Business Value Validation Matrix
- All quantified financial impacts are presented with confidence intervals, empirical evidence sources, and dual executive verification.
