# Enterprise Commercial & Revenue Governance Policy 🏛️💼
**Document Identifier**: `JUR-POL-COMM-2026-V21`  
**Target Release**: `v21.0.0 — Commercial Intelligence & Customer Trust`  
**Effective Date**: `August 26, 2026`  
**Classification**: `Institutional Executive Directive`  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy governs commercial intelligence modeling, customer trust transparency, knowledge pack distribution, and revenue analytics for enterprise deployments of JurisTech Solutions.

---

## 2. Inviolable Commercial Guardrails 🔒
1. **Financial Estimation Disclaimers**: All ARR, LTV, and expansion projections generated by the platform are empirical estimations and do not constitute binding financial warranties (`FINANCIAL_ESTIMATION_ONLY = true`).
2. **Zero Customer Document Training**: Client contracts, proprietary clauses, and sensitive negotiation records are strictly prohibited from being utilized to train or curate marketplace packs (`NO_CLIENT_DOCUMENT_TRAINING = true`, `NO_CUSTOMER_DATA_USED_FOR_REVENUE_TRAINING = true`).
3. **No Autonomous Sales or Pricing Actions**: AI engines are restricted to telemetry analysis and recommendation generation (`NO_AUTONOMOUS_SALES_DECISION = true`, `NO_AUTOMATED_PRICE_CHANGE = true`).
4. **Public Trust Integrity**: The Customer Trust Portal provides cryptographic proof of certifications (ISO 27001, ISO 42001, SOC 2, SDAIA, NCA) without disclosing internal code or confidential backend infrastructure (`ZERO_INTERNAL_CODE_EXPOSURE = true`, `ZERO_CUSTOMER_DATA_EXPOSURE = true`).

---

## 3. Executive Oversight & Dual Sign-Off
Commercial analytics and revenue realization reports require explicit dual sign-off from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and General Counsel (GC) prior to institutional dissemination.
