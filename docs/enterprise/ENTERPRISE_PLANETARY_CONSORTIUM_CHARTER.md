## 1. Governance Principles of the Planetary Legal Consortium
1. **Multi-Lateral Consensus**: No single state, corporation, or entity can unilaterally dictate consortium standards or policy changes (`CONSORTIUM_CONSENSUS_REQUIRED = true`).
2. **Equal Voting Weight**: Every sovereign institutional member possesses strictly equal voting power (`votingWeight = 1.0`), precluding pay-to-play or corporate dominance (`NO_DOMINANT_ENTITY = true`).
3. **In-Country Sovereign Residency**: Inter-node collaboration is achieved strictly through zero-knowledge mathematical proofs; raw legal briefs and customer contracts never leave sovereign borders (`IN_COUNTRY_DATA_RESIDENCY_INVIOLABLE = true`).
4. **Human Judicial Primacy**: All consortium resolutions, code updates, and adoption graduations require explicit, named human signatures.
