## 1. Operational Maturity Mission
The Operational Maturity Charter formalizes the progression of JurisTech Solutions from an enterprise-ready architecture into a live, scalable operational ecosystem delivering measurable institutional legal value across sovereign borders.

---

## 2. Inviolable Operational Guardrails 🔒
1. **Zero Customer Data Exploitation**: No client or partner documents are stored or leveraged for commercial monetization (`ZERO_CLIENT_DATA_EXPOSURE = true`).
2. **Explainable Trust & Performance Metrics**: All evaluations must provide transparent, human-auditable rationale, forbidding arbitrary or opaque algorithmic ranking (`NO_ALGORITHMIC_BLACKLISTING = true`).
3. **Mandatory Human Executive Authorization**: All partner activations, cross-border federations, and AI governance certifications require explicit human executive countersignatures (`DUAL_HUMAN_REGISTRATION_APPROVAL = true`).
