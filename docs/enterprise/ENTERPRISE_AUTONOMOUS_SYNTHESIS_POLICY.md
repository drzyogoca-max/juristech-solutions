## 1. Scope & Core Objectives
This policy defines the governance boundaries, operational constraints, and supervisory oversight required for all autonomous institutional syntheses generated across JurisTech Solutions federated legal nodes.

---

## 2. Mandatory Boundary Rules

1. **Advisory Intelligence Only**:
   - Syntheses provide comparative statutory analysis, precedent mapping, and regulatory trend aggregation.
   - Syntheses shall NEVER constitute legal counsel, binding judicial interpretation, or autonomous decisions.

2. **Mandatory Human Legal Oversight**:
   - Every synthesized dossier must be formally reviewed and signed off by a qualified Chief Legal Officer or accredited partner before publication.

3. **Official Gazette Anchoring**:
   - Every comparative point must be anchored to verifiable official gazettes, royal decrees, or parliamentary enactments.

4. **Sandbox Simulation Isolation**:
   - All predictive stress-testing simulations must execute in isolated sandbox environments with zero ability to modify production rules.
