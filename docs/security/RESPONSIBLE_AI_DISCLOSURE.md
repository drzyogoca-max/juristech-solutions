# JurisTech Solutions — Responsible AI & Security Vulnerability Disclosure Policy
**Version**: 1.0.0 (v15.0+ Series)  
**Standard**: ISO/IEC 29147 Vulnerability Disclosure, ISO/IEC 30111 Vulnerability Handling Processes, FIRST PSIRT Framework

---

## 1. Safe Harbor & Researcher Commitment

JurisTech Solutions is dedicated to maintaining the highest level of security, algorithmic fairness, and zero-knowledge privacy. We welcome responsible security researchers, academic cryptographers, and enterprise auditors to report potential security or algorithmic bias vulnerabilities.

### Safe Harbor Principles
- **No Legal Action**: If you follow this policy in good faith, JurisTech will not initiate or support legal action against you.
- **Privacy Protection**: Do not access, modify, or retain customer data or private tenant memory.
- **Coordination & Confidentiality**: Give JurisTech reasonable time (up to 90 days) to mitigate the issue before public disclosure.
